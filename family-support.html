You Are Not Alone

A DIPG diagnosis changes everything in an instant. Our goal is to lighten the load in whatever way we can — answering questions, pointing you to trusted resources, and simply being present.

If you or someone you love is facing DIPG, please reach out. We would be honored to walk alongside you.
