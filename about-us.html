Our story

The Brave Journey of Ronil That Inspired Our Mission

Ronil was a resilient and compassionate teenager whose bravery in facing DIPG inspired a mission to support families and advance research.

Who We Are

The Brave Ronil Foundation aids children with DIPG through research, family support, and awareness — helping families face challenges and work toward better treatments.

Our foundation was born from Ronil's courage in the face of an impossible diagnosis. His spirit lives on in every family we stand beside, every trial we help fund, and every conversation that brings DIPG out of the shadows.

What drives us

Our Mission in Three Pillars

Funding research, supporting families & raising awareness.

Research

Supporting innovative research and clinical trials to improve treatment and outcomes for children with DIPG.

Family Support

We offer resources, support, and guidance for families dealing with a DIPG diagnosis.

Awareness

We advocate for DIPG awareness and urgent research through outreach and social involvement.

Milan Mehta, CEO
