## A. STATISTICS & FACTS — corrections

### A1. The homepage / About DIPG "stats band" (three tiles) — all three need fixing

The current tiles read: **"2 min — A Child is Diagnosed"**, **"#1 — Leading Cause of Death"**,
**"< 1% — Less Than 1% Survival."** As written, each is imprecise or misleading. Replace as follows.

**Tile 1 — "Every 2 minutes"**
- **Problem:** "Every two minutes a child is diagnosed with cancer" is a **worldwide** figure
  (WHO/IARC, via St. Baldrick's, 2016). It is **not** a U.S. number. Presenting it next to a
  U.S.-based foundation's messaging conflates a global stat with a U.S. one.
- **CHANGE TO (keep the tile, add one word + a footnote):**
  > **Every 2 minutes** — *A child is diagnosed with cancer **worldwide***
- **Or, if you prefer a U.S. figure:**
  > **~15,000 a year** — *U.S. children and teens diagnosed with cancer (about one every 35 minutes)*
- **Source:** WHO/IARC via St. Baldrick's (2016); NCI *Cancer in Children and Adolescents* (2024); ACS/Siegel, *Cancer Statistics 2025*.

**Tile 2 — "#1 Leading Cause of Death"**
- **Problem:** Cancer is **not** the overall leading cause of death in children — accidents/injuries
  are. Cancer is the leading cause of death **by disease**. Without that qualifier the claim is inaccurate.
- **CHANGE TO:**
  > **#1** — *Leading cause of death **by disease** in U.S. children (past infancy)*
- **Source:** NCI *Cancer in Children and Adolescents* (2024/2025). (Overall-cause context: NEJM, Cunningham et al., 2018.)

**Tile 3 — "< 1% Survival"**
- **Problem:** "<1%" refers specifically to **5-year** survival. On its own it reads as if
  *no* child survives, and it omits the number families most need — median survival in months.
- **CHANGE TO (recommended — make it DIPG-specific and honest):**
  > **8–11 months** — *Median survival after a DIPG diagnosis*
  > …and, in the body copy beneath the band, add:
  > *"Fewer than 1 in 10 children live two years, and the 5-year survival rate is only about 1–2%."*
- **Source:** International & SIOPE DIPG Registry pooled analysis (Hoffman et al., *J Clin Oncol* 2018; median OS 11 months, 2-yr 9.6%, 5-yr 2.2%); St. Jude; ChadTough Defeat DIPG.

### A2. Verified DIPG/DMG numbers — use these anywhere else on the site

Where the site states any DIPG fact (especially the **About DIPG** page), use these figures and
always state **(a)** U.S. vs. worldwide, **(b)** the time window, and **(c)** keep them consistent sitewide.

| Fact | Use this | Source |
|---|---|---|
| DIPG diagnoses per year (U.S.) | **~300 children/year** (range 150–300) | NCI; St. Jude; Children's Hospital LA |
| DIPG diagnoses worldwide | **Do not publish a specific number** — no reliable global count exists | (flagged uncertain) |
| Median age at diagnosis | **6–7 years** (typical range 5–10) | International DIPG/DMG Registry |
| Median overall survival | **~8–11 months from diagnosis** | Hoffman et al., JCO 2018 |
| 2-year survival | **~10% (fewer than 1 in 10)** | St. Jude; DIPG Registry |
| 5-year survival | **~1–2%** | Hoffman et al., 2018 |
| Surgery | **Not possible** — DIPG grows diffusely in the brainstem; biopsy only | NCI; St. Jude |
| Standard of care | **Radiation** — eases symptoms in ~80% of children but is **not a cure** | St. Jude; DIPG.org |
| Rank | **Leading cause of brain-tumor death in children** | StatPearls 2024; CBTRUS |
| DMG / mutation | DIPG is now classified as **diffuse midline glioma (DMG), H3 K27-altered**; **~80–85%** carry the H3 K27M mutation | WHO 2021; ABTA; PMC11120159 (2024) |
| Prognosis unchanged for | **~5 decades (about 50 years)** | Damodharan & Puccetti, 2023 |

### A3. Do NOT use (unsupported or misleading)

- ❌ A specific **worldwide DIPG** annual count — no reliable published figure exists.
- ❌ A **DIPG-specific federal funding percentage** — none exists. If a funding stat is used,
  the only defensible one is **"~4% of the U.S. National Cancer Institute budget goes to *all*
  pediatric cancers combined"** — clearly labeled as all pediatric cancer, not DIPG.
- ❌ "Every 2 (or 3) minutes" presented as a **U.S.** figure — it is worldwide.
- ❌ A bare **"<1% survival"** with no time window.
- ⚠️ "Only X drugs developed for children in Y years" type lines — vary widely across advocacy
  sites; source independently or drop.

> peacefully at home. Honoring his final wishes, his family donated his tumor to Stanford for DIPG
> research; it became one of the only Wild-Type cell lines used in DIPG research and trials
> worldwide. Through that gift, Ronil's legacy reaches far beyond his own journey — offering hope

## C. FORWARD-LOOKING FRAMING — required for Family Support and all programs

**This is important and applies sitewide.** The Brave Ronil Foundation is a **new, independent
organization.** It has not yet run programs, funded grants, or delivered family services. Any copy
that describes these as things we *already do* is inaccurate. All program content must be written
in **forward-looking language** — what families **will be able to** access as we build these programs.

### C1. Family Support page

- **First, remove the "Expert Guidance / calls with leading doctors" section** (already requested —
  still live as of Aug 3). It is written in the present tense ("We organize one-on-one calls…") and
  implies an established service we are not yet running.
- **Rewrite the Family Support offerings in the future tense.** Use this framing:
  > *"The Brave Ronil Foundation is building a family-support program so that families facing DIPG
  > **will be able to** find guidance, connect with resources, and never feel alone in the journey."*
- **Sentence template for each offering** — always "families will be able to…":
  - *"Families **will be able to** access curated resources for navigating diagnosis, treatment options, and second opinions."*
  - *"Families **will be able to** connect with support that helps ease the practical and emotional weight of a DIPG diagnosis."*
  - *"We **are working to** provide families with clear, trustworthy information at the moment they need it most."*
- **Avoid:** "We organize," "We provide," "We connect you with the world's top doctors," "Our program
  supports…" — anything that states an active, delivered service.

### C2. Research / Impact page

- Present research funding as **future intent**: *"The Brave Ronil Foundation **will fund** research
  grants and clinical trials that bring us closer to a cure."* Do **not** list previously funded
  grants as BRF's own.
- Any past accomplishments shown on the Impact page must be attributed to the **co-founders' prior
  work** (past tense, neutral phrasing such as "before founding the Brave Ronil Foundation") — never
  as BRF's record. (See the earlier change request, item 5.)

### C3. Everywhere else

- When in doubt, ask: *"Has BRF actually done this yet?"* If not, write it as **"we will,"** **"we're
  building,"** or **"families will be able to."**
- BRF's own future plans can be shown clearly labeled as such — e.g., a **"What we're working
  toward"** or **"Our first initiatives"** section — kept visually distinct from anything historical.
