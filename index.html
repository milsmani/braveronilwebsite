Ronil's story
His brave journey

The Brave Journey of Ronil That Inspired Our Mission

Ronil was a resilient and compassionate teenager whose bravery in facing DIPG inspired a mission to support families and advance research.

His courage in the hardest of circumstances became the heartbeat of this foundation. Today, every program we run carries his spirit forward — turning grief into purpose and purpose into hope for the next family.
